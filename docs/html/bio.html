What Drives Me

Understanding Complex Systems

I'm fascinated by the engineering challenges that emerge when software must perform reliably under real constraints. Through my studies in distributed systems and high-performance computing, I have become particularly interested in how architecture, concurrency, and resource management shape a system's performance.

Software in the Physical World

Aviation has fascinated me for as long as I can remember. I am captivated by the engineering that makes flight possible, as well as the experience of piloting itself, a fascination that led me to obtain my private pilot's license in college. I'm drawn to the technology behind aircraft, aerospace systems, and the infrastructure that connects people and places. I want to work on projects where the connection between engineering decisions and real-world capabilities is tangible.

Engineering with Purpose

I value software that is dependable and built to last. I enjoy exploring not only whether a system works, but why it works, where its limitations lie, and how it can be made more robust. I hope to bring that curiosity and care to challenging engineering problems alongside people who take pride in the quality of their work.
